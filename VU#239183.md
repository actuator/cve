# Slimpal WiFi Bluetooth Smart Body Scale - Unencrypted Transmission of Sensitive Data

> **Disclosure Notice:** The information provided in this document is for educational purposes only and was initially reported to the vendor through coordinated disclosure requests. The vendor did not respond.

![image](https://github.com/actuator/cve/assets/78701239/7e4454ea-0a59-4b10-b470-115bc0549c10)

## Product Information

**Product Name:** Slimpal WiFi Bluetooth Smart Body Scale

**CWE:** CWE-319: Cleartext Transmission of Sensitive Information

**Affected Component(s):**

* **Model:** CS20S
* **FCC ID:** 2ANDX-CS20S
* **Android App:** `Slimpal`
* **Affected Version:** 2.9.0

**Vendor/Project:** Slimpal

## Vulnerability Description

A vulnerability has been discovered in the Bluetooth-enabled Slimpal CS20S Smart Body Scale that results in the unencrypted transmission of personally identifiable and sensitive information between the scale and the corresponding mobile device running the Slimpal Android application version 2.9.0.

Observed information includes:

* Age
* Date of Birth
* Gender
* Weight
* Height

The information can be recovered from captured Bluetooth Low Energy communications between the scale and the mobile application.

![vrf23-01-JXSBZ\_scalePcap](https://github.com/actuator/cve/assets/78701239/f2c45385-0e5c-437c-9fdf-d450e23190ef)

## Exploit

This vulnerability exposes sensitive information to unauthorized parties within Bluetooth signal range by allowing an attacker to sniff unencrypted BLE traffic exchanged between the Slimpal CS20S scale and its associated Android application.

## Impact

Successful exploitation could expose personally identifiable and sensitive body-measurement information to an unauthorized party within Bluetooth reception range.

The disclosed information could potentially be used for user profiling, tracking, or other privacy-invasive activities.

## Discovery

The affected device was purchased and subsequently analyzed for security issues.

BLE packet captures were collected during communication between the Slimpal CS20S Smart Body Scale and the Slimpal Android application. Analysis of the captured traffic identified sensitive user information being transmitted without adequate confidentiality protections.
